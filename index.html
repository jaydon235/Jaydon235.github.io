About

For four years I've worked frontline healthcare roles — CNA-certified since August 2022 — on travel contracts across Oregon, Montana, Texas, and Florida, 15+ assignments in all. Every new contract meant learning a new facility's EHR/EMR system fast and handling patient data under HIPAA on every shift. I'm currently full-time at the Oregon Veteran's Home in The Dalles, OR. That's where the interest in security actually started — not from a textbook, but from watching how much trust gets placed in systems that patients and staff rarely think about.

I'm now finishing an Associate's degree in Cybersecurity at Portland Community College, expected June 2027. Alongside that I earned a Cybersecurity Foundations certificate in June 2026 and I'm currently working through CompTIA Network+ and Security+, with CCNA coursework starting this fall.

My first move is an entry-level IT support or help desk role — I want the hands-on troubleshooting reps. Longer term, I'm aiming at security work in healthcare: GRC and compliance roles where my background on the clinical side is actually an advantage, not just a career change. I'm also targeting hands-on programs like the DoW Cyber Registered Apprenticeship Program to build that experience directly.
